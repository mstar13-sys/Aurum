Questions

Everything You'd Think to Ask

Our most frequently asked questions, answered clearly.

Standard check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is by 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in and late check-out are available on request, subject to availability. Guaranteed early arrival from 9:00 AM can be pre-arranged for an additional fee.

Reservations may be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival. Cancellations within 48 hours will incur a one-night charge. Non-refundable rate bookings cannot be cancelled or modified.

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, JCB), bank transfers, and PayPal. A credit card is required to secure your reservation. Payment is processed in full 24 hours before arrival.

Yes. We offer complimentary valet parking for all guests. Self-parking is also available in our secure underground facility. Oversized and RV parking requires advance notice.

We welcome well-behaved dogs up to 15 kg in our Garden Wing rooms. A refundable pet deposit of $100 is required. Pets are not permitted in dining, spa, or pool areas. Please contact us before booking to confirm availability.

Room upgrade requests can be made during booking or by contacting your dedicated Guest Relations Officer. Complimentary upgrades are occasionally offered to returning guests and loyalty members, subject to availability on arrival day.
